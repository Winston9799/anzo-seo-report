✓ Wins This Month

  • Not-indexed pages fell 65% — from ~6,840 (Feb) to 2,417 by end of April. Massive crawl budget recovery.
  • 5xx server errors down 87% — from 638 to 83 issues. Backend stability greatly improved.
  • FAQ rich results surged — from 1–2 valid results in Feb to 30 by late April.
  • Breadcrumb rich results grew — now 31 valid URLs with zero invalid, up from 1 in February.
  • ai.anzocapital.com breakout — 199 clicks in April vs only 35 in March (+469%).
  • No-deposit bonus content gaining traction — blog page up from 4 to 56 clicks (+1,300%).
  • Non-branded clicks grew 16.7% MoM — content strategy is starting to deliver.

⚠ Concerns to Address

  • Average position worsened — from 7.1 to 8.2 despite more impressions; dilution from new lower-ranking content.
  • Mobile LCP is critical — 261 URLs with Poor LCP (>4s); zero good-LCP URLs detected.
  • Mobile INP needs improvement — 261 URLs flagged (>200ms); interaction responsiveness lagging.
  • 778 duplicate canonical issues — largest coverage problem, though down from 932 last month.
  • 1,323 pages crawled but not indexed — Google is visiting but not trusting these pages.
  • Non-branded CTR only 0.49% — 161K+ impressions converting poorly; titles & meta need optimisation.
  • my.anzocapital.com clicks fell 45% — login portal losing visibility; position held steady, something changed.
